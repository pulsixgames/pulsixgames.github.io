An Important Announcement Regarding the Website

After much consideration, I have made the difficult decision to officially shut down the game website. What started as a small project eventually grew into something that many students throughout the school came to enjoy. It has been incredibly rewarding to see so many people use the website, and I genuinely appreciate everyone who visited, supported it, and helped make it what it became. Unfortunately, due to the circumstances surrounding the school's restrictions and concerns about students accessing games during school hours, I believe shutting down the website is the most appropriate decision. While this isn't necessarily how I wanted things to end, I feel it is the right thing to do. I understand that many of you can most likely find alternative ways to access the website, even after its shutdown. However, I strongly discourage attempting to do so. Please respect this decision and, more importantly, prioritize your education. School is ultimately a place to learn, and I encourage everyone to stay focused on their assignments and make the most of their time in the classroom. Finally, I want to sincerely thank everyone who used the website and made this project worthwhile. What began as a simple idea turned into something much bigger than I initially expected, and I'm grateful that I was able to share it with all of you. Thank you for everything. - Sincerely, NKC
